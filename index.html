our story

Made for moments worth savoring.

We believe coffee is more than a drink—it's a ritual, a conversation starter, and an escape from the everyday. Maria's Coffee House was imagined to bring people together through quality, warmth, and timeless elegance.

Founded with a passion for exceptional coffee and hospitality, we've become Doha's go-to destination for those who appreciate the finer things.

  • Handcrafted coffee prepared with precision and care
  • Elegant interiors designed for comfort and style
  • Perfect for meetings, catch-ups, and quiet reflection
  • A community of coffee lovers and friends
